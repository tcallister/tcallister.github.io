With hundreds of gravitational-wave detections on hand, we now have sufficient data to conduct a **large-scale census** of the black hole and neutron star population,
asking questions like:

* What is the overall distribution of compact object masses?
* How rapidly do compact objects spin, and in which directions?
* And how does the prevalence of compact binary mergers evolve over cosmic time?

These and other questions are answered via careful statistical inference with large, noisy, and incomplete catalogs of gravitational-wave data.
