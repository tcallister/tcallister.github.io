Although we now have observations of many hundreds of black hole mergers, the astrophysical environments in which these black holes form and the processes driving them to merger remain unknown.
By studying the observed properties of merging black holes, either the characteristics of individual outliers or the collective traits of the black hole population, we can try to learn where and how black holes are born, the details of their environments, and maybe even something about their stellar progenitors.
