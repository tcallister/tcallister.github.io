The total masses and redshifts of all binary black hole detections published by the LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA Collaboration through GWTC-3.
I like using this pair of plots to illustrate why analysis of the compact binary population is hard.
If you look only at point estimates of binary properties (first plot), it seems as if there is a clear evolution of black hole masses with redshift.
Conclusions are much murkier if you instead look at full posteriors on binary masses and redshifts (second plot); this is further compounded by search selection effects (which prevent observations in the bottom right corner) and finite observing time (consistent with no observations in the top left corner).
