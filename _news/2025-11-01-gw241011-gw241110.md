I'm thrilled to announce the [publication](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.3847/2041-8213/ae0d54) of a full-collaboration paper I principally authored, describing a pair of binary black holes, GW241011 and GW241110, with remarkable spin properties.
These two binaries are mirror images of one another, both in their names and in their spin properties.
GW241011 possesses one of the most rapid and aligned black holes observed to date, whereas GW241110 is among the first events to exhibit an "upside down" black hole spinning counter to its orbit.
