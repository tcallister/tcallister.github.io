My [recent paper](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevX.14.021005) with Will was featured in Physics Magazine, with a wonderful ["Viewpoint" article](https://physics.aps.org/articles/v17/57) written by Maya Fishbach.
In her article, Maya pedagogically lays out the methodological differences between traditional parametric vs. "non-parametric" models and describes some highlights from our own work.
Thank you, Maya!
